Community firmware for the Quansheng UV-K5 (V3), UV-K1 and UV-K1(8)

Karina adds a spectrum analyser, FM broadcast radio, flexible scanning and a reworked menu to the radio you already own. Free, open licence, no accounts.

Before you flash anything

Firmware built for one hardware version bricks another one. Check which radio you have, and back up your calibration first — it is unique to your radio and nobody can give it back to you.

Which radio do I have?#

Switch the radio off, hold PTT and switch it on: the display shows the bootloader version. Its first digit tells you the hardware version.

Bootloader starts withRadioKarina build
2, 3, 4, 5UV-K5 V1karina-v1 (separate repository)
1UV-K5 V2not supported
7UV-K5 V3, UV-K1, UV-K1(8)this repository

V1 radios carry no marking; V2 and V3 usually have one on the sticker under the battery.

Got the older UV-K5 V1? It has a build of its own, in a separate repository: autumn1rain/karina-v1

What Karina adds#

Spectrum analyser

Band presets, a free-entry mode, live capture of the active frequency, a blacklist for noisy channels, and controls for attenuator, AGC and squelch.

Scanning that behaves

Scan lists, priority channels checked during the scan, and any channel skipped on the fly by switching its attenuator on.

Subtone scan

Scan CTCSS/DCS tones on the current frequency and store the one that was found as the transmit subtone.

FM broadcast radio

Station search with memories, resumed automatically five seconds after a signal on the working channel disappears.

Attenuator in three steps

ATT1, ATT2 and ATT3 per frequency or channel — useful next to a strong transmitter and for skipping channels while scanning.

Power, battery and extras

HIGH / MID / LOW TX power, a reworked battery saver, dynamic volume, a frequency and power randomizer, NOAA, AM and USB modulation.

Install#

The short version. The full procedure with screenshots is in the installation guide below.

  1. Back up your calibration and configuration while the radio still runs its current firmware.
  2. Switch the radio off, hold PTT and switch it on — that is flash mode.
  3. Flash the file for your radio with any of the tools below.
  4. Clear the memory: switch off, then switch on holding PTT + EXIT for about two seconds.
  5. Write your calibration back, then write the Karina calibration from the release.
  6. Reset the radio: MENU → RESET → VFO.

Questions#

Can I flash this into a UV-K5 V1?

No. V1 has a different processor and a different bootloader; the wrong firmware bricks the radio. V1 owners need the karina-v1 build.

Do I really need the calibration backup?

Yes. Calibration holds your own radio's TX power and battery data. It is not in any release and nobody can restore it for you.

Can I keep my settings from another firmware?

No. Only a configuration saved from an earlier Karina version can be written back. Channels can be moved with CHIRP.

The radio asks for a code after I wrote settings from CHIRP.

That happens when a side key is left as NONE. Fill in all four side-key assignments (short and long press for both keys) before writing.

Where do I ask for help?

In the Telegram chat, in the comments. Please say which radio and which firmware version you have.
